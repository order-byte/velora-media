# AG Archviz Studio — Ad Script v1

A ~73-second, 9:16 motion-graphics ad (voiceover and on-screen text, no on-camera talent) that follows the reference ad's flow, opens on a sharper hook, and names Mr. Ayush as the architect-engineer behind the studio.

Shareable doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/AWpZD5eLMDtCYbzbqCHUzk

## Brief

| Item | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Format | 9:16 vertical, motion graphics + voiceover, no model or presenter on camera |
| Length | ~73 s at a natural pace (reference ad: 82 s) |
| Audience | Architects, interior designers and studio owners overloaded with 3D and drawing work |
| Markets | Main market is the US, but the ad runs in several countries: no country, currency or unit is named in the video |
| Structure | Awareness (hook lands in the first second) → Valuable information → Problem solving → Promotion |
| Voice | Neutral international English, confident and conversational, about 160 words per minute |
| On-screen text | US spelling (modeling, color); every key line is on screen so the ad works with sound off |
| Client name | Mr. Ayush is named in the voiceover and on a founder card |

## Voiceover script (~197 words)

### 1. Awareness — the hook (0:00–0:05)

**"Architects — your competition isn't better than you.**
**They just have a team you never see."**

### 2. Valuable information (0:05–0:20)

"Here's what busy studios don't advertise: much of their 3D and drawing work isn't done in-house.
A back-end team handles it, while the principal wins the next project.
Because clients don't fall for floor plans. They fall for what they can see."

### 3. Problem solving — the problem (0:20–0:34)

"And you? You win the project… then vanish into it.
Modeling at midnight. Redlines at 2 a.m.
Your 3D artist quits. The new hire needs months of training.
And every hour you spend drafting is an hour you're not billing."

### 3. Problem solving — the solution (0:34–1:01)

"That's exactly why architect and engineer **Mr. Ayush** built **AG Archviz Studio**: your back-end design team.
You share the brief. We handle the rest.
3D modeling and photoreal renders. Layout planning and drafting. Structural and working drawings. For architecture and interiors.
And because it's run by an architect-engineer, your drawings don't just look good on screen. They work on site.
No hiring. No salaries. No training. No chasing staff.
Just on-time delivery, while you focus on winning clients."

### 4. Promotion (1:01–1:13)

"Start with one project, and see how stress-free it feels.
Tap the button below and message AG Archviz Studio today.
**Your competition already has a hidden team.**
**Now you do too."**

## Alternate hooks

For A/B testing, swap only the first 5 seconds; each one drops straight into the Valuable information beat.

| Angle | Voiceover | First-second visual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main: ego + curiosity | "Architects — your competition isn't better than you. They just have a team you never see." | "ARCHITECTS." on black, then the split-screen render |
| Loss | "Your client just said yes… to another architect's render." | A phone pops up "Contract signed" with someone else's render on it |
| Command | "Architects: stop drawing your own projects. The busiest studios already have." | A pencil snaps mid-line on a floor plan |
| Late-night pain | "It's 2 a.m. Why is the architect still rendering?" | A clock hits 2:00 AM over one glowing monitor in a dark office |

If the client wants only one, keep the main hook: it is the only one the closing line ("Now you do too") pays off.

## Reference ad vs. our script

| Beat | Reference ad (Wallarc, 82 s, presenter on camera, Hindi) | Our script (73 s, motion graphics, English) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | Plain call-out: "If you're an architect, interior designer or studio owner…" | Call-out plus provocation: "Your competition isn't better than you." |
| Setup | Projects come in every week, but you handle payments and site work | An insight first: busy studios get their 3D and drawings from a back-end team |
| Pain | You make the 3D yourself and finish drawings at night | Same, in four short lines: midnight modeling, 2 a.m. redlines |
| Excuses | No staff, 3D person quit, stuck on client selections, new team needs training | Kept, cut to the two strongest: artist quits, new hire needs training |
| Turn | "What are smart architects doing? Handing the workload to a back-end team" | Same idea, set up by the hook and paid off here |
| Offer | Share the brief, we handle the rest; list of services; on time | Same, plus Mr. Ayush as architect-engineer: "looks right on screen, works on site" |
| Benefits | No hiring, no salary, no tension; you focus on clients | Same rhythm, as a stamped list |
| CTA | Click the link, chat on WhatsApp, try one project together | "Start with one project", a button that works with any channel, callback close |

## To confirm with the client

- [ ] **Brand name as spoken:** is it "AG Archviz Studio"? (The logo shows the AG monogram above "Archviz Studio".)
- [ ] **Mr. Ayush's title on screen:** "Architect" is a licensed title in many markets, so the founder card should match his actual qualification.
- [ ] **Full name:** keep "Mr. Ayush", or add his surname? Mr. plus a first name can sound unusual to Western audiences.
- [ ] **Services:** add or drop anything (walkthrough animations, BIM, permit or construction sets?).
- [ ] **First-project offer:** free, discounted or paid? The script only says "start with one project".
- [ ] **CTA destination:** WhatsApp, website form or booked call. "Tap the button below" works with any of them.
- [ ] **Turnaround:** if they have a standard delivery time, replace "on-time delivery" with the real number.
- [ ] **Founder photo or signature:** optional, but it adds trust to the founder card without a shoot.
