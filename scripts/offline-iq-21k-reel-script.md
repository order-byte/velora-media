# Reel Script — "₹21,000 From One 60-Sec Video (US Client)"

**Client:** Offline IQ (offlineiq.ai), a US company. They make private AI that runs inside a company's own office, with no internet.
**Length:** under 60 sec (VO ≈ 140 words, about 55 sec)
**Language:** ~60% English, ~40% Hindi (Hindi written in English letters)

**The story in one line:** US client reaches out → meeting → details on WhatsApp → website research → deal ₹20,000 + ₹10,000 advance → After Effects + Premiere → delivery + one change → final ₹11,000 (₹1,000 extra) → total ₹21,000.

## WHAT YOU NEED

**Face cam (2 shots):** the hook and the CTA. Phone vertical at eye level, light from a window in front of you.

**Screenshots / screen recordings:**
1. The client's first message
2. The meeting screenshot
3. The WhatsApp chat with the details
4. A screen recording of offlineiq.ai (slow scroll)
5. The ₹10,000 advance payment
6. After Effects + Premiere Pro scene: no screen recording needed. The editor recreates both interfaces with the real Offline IQ video playing at 2× (see `offline-iq-editor-brief.md`)
7. The finished Offline IQ video file (used inside that scene)
8. The delivery message + the client's change request
9. The ₹11,000 final payment

---

## KEEP IN MIND

- **Blur** client names, faces in the meeting screenshot, phone numbers, emails and transaction IDs, unless the client said it's okay to show them.
- **Captions on every word.** Many people watch with the sound off.
- **Speak with a bit more energy than normal.** What feels "too much" while recording sounds normal in the reel.
- **Record the VO line by line**, 2–3 takes each, and keep the best take.
- **If it runs over 60 sec,** shorten the "Making" part first.
