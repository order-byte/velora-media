## Scene notes
- **Hook:** something must move and text must be on screen from the very first frame. No intro, no logo. Punch-zoom on "₹21,000".
- **Screenshots:** never leave one sitting still. Slowly zoom or pan, and highlight the line that matters (the message, the amount).
- **Website:** zoom in on their headline, "Your AI. Inside your walls. Nothing leaves."
- **Making (scene 7):** speed up the screen recordings, cut on the music beat, and end on ~1 sec of the finished video if I've attached it.
- **Payment (scene 9):** this is the climax. Animate a number counter, split ₹11,000 into ₹10,000 + ₹1,000 extra, leave a short silence before "Total", then an impact sound.
- **CTA:** end on a hard cut so the reel loops cleanly.
- **Highlight colour:** if the reference doesn't set one, use #C77255 (Offline IQ's orange) for key words in captions.

## Rules
1. Under 60 seconds.
2. Blur private info: client names, faces in the meeting screenshot, phone numbers, emails, transaction IDs, account numbers. Keep the amounts visible.
3. Caption every spoken word. Keep text inside the safe zone: not in the top ~250 px, not in the bottom ~420 px, not on the right edge where Instagram's buttons sit.
4. The amounts must always match: ₹20,000 deal, ₹10,000 advance, ₹11,000 final, ₹1,000 extra, ₹21,000 total.
5. Don't add claims or on-screen text that aren't in this brief.
6. If the reference style conflicts with the scene notes, follow the reference. Rules 1–5 always apply.
