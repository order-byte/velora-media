## Upload checklist
1. Instagram: Settings → Data usage and media quality → turn ON "Upload at highest quality".
2. Move the file to the phone via Google Drive / USB / AirDrop. Not WhatsApp or Telegram as a video (they compress it); if WhatsApp, send as Document.
3. Upload on stable Wi-Fi.
4. In Instagram: no filter, no trimming, no extra text or Instagram music (the hidden frame and the mix stay exactly as made).
5. Cover: "Edit cover" → "Add from camera roll" → `aminur_hidden_frame_cover.jpg`. It is laid out so the 3:4 grid crop still reads "I HID ONE FRAME".
6. Keep "Share to Feed" on. Rename the audio to `I hid one frame · Aminur`.
7. Add location (your city), and Topics if Instagram shows the option.

## After posting (first 60 minutes)
- Reply to every comment, especially frame-number guesses ("close!", "try again 👀"); replies keep the thread growing.
- Story: share the reel with a Quiz sticker "Which frame is hidden?" (4 frame numbers).
- Send it to 5 to 10 past clients or friends who will actually watch it fully.
- Don't delete and re-upload if early views are slow; Reels often pick up after 24 to 48 hours.

## Best time
Weekday evening, 7 to 9 PM IST, but check Insights → Total followers → Most active times and post about 30 minutes before your own peak.
