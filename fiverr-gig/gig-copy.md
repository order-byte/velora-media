**FAQ** (9 questions, every answer under 300 characters)

1. **Can I see more of your work before I order?**
   Yes. The gig video has clips from recent client projects. Send me a message and I'll share full videos that match your industry.

2. **What do you need from me to start?**
   Your website or app link, your logo, fonts and colors, and screenshots or screen recordings of the product. If you already have a script, send that too.

3. **Can you animate our real product UI?**
   Yes, most of our work is built from real interfaces. We rebuild your screens in After Effects so they stay sharp in 4K and move smoothly, instead of just zooming into a screen recording.

4. **How many revisions do I get?**
   2, 3 or 5 depending on the package. Script and storyboard changes are free until you approve them. A script change after animation starts means a new voice over, so that part is charged separately.

5. **Can you deliver faster? My launch is soon.**
   Yes. Add the extra fast delivery option when you order. If you have a fixed launch date, message me first so I can confirm we have the time.

6. **Do I get the source file?**
   Yes. The After Effects project is included in Premium, and you can add it as an extra to Basic or Standard.

7. **Can I get versions for social media?**
   Yes. We can cut the same video to 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, or 1:1 for LinkedIn. Add the vertical version extra or ask me for a custom offer.

8. **I need a length that isn't 30, 60 or 90 seconds. Is that okay?**
   No problem. Message me with the length and some details about your product, and I'll send you a custom offer.

9. **Can I share a reference video I like?**
   Please do. References help a lot. Tell us what you like about them (the pacing, the style, the colors) and we'll use that as the starting point for your video.
