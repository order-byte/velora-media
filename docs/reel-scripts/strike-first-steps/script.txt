Copyright strike aaya, ab pehle kya karein (reel, under 1 minute)

1. AWARENESS (opening, pick one)

Option 1: Ek din YouTube ka email aayega, "Aapke channel pe copyright strike laga hai." Us din aap kya karoge? Bahut log ghabra ke galti kar baithte hain.

Option 2: Main chahta hoon ki yeh email aapke paas kabhi na aaye. Lekin agar aa gaya, toh yeh pachaas second aapke bahut kaam aayenge.

Option 3: Copyright strike aaya? Ruko. Abhi kuch bhi delete mat karna. Pehle yeh suno.

2. VALUABLE INFORMATION

Strike matlab, owner ki shikayat par aapka video hata, aur channel pe ek nishaan lag gaya. Nabbe din mein teen strike, toh channel band ho sakta hai.

3. PROBLEM SOLVING

Ab kya karein? Teen kaam.

Ek, email poora padho. Kabhi-kabhi usmein saat din ka mauka hota hai, strike se bachne ka.

Do, wahi video dobara upload mat karo. Doosra strike aa sakta hai.

Teen, sahi raasta chuno. Copyright School karke nabbe din ruko, owner se strike wapas lene ko kaho, ya video aapka hai toh counter notification.

4. PROMOTION

Kaunsa raasta sahi hai, yeh har case mein alag hai. "STRIKE" likh ke DM karo. Hum aapka case dekhkar sach-sach batayenge.
