# Reel script: Copyright Claim vs Strike

Value reel, about 52 seconds (max 1 minute), with a hook that lands in under 3 seconds.
Rules from the client: simple words, no jargon, friendly tone, researched facts.

Voiceover demos: `Claim-vs-Strike-Hindi-VO.mp3` (male voice) and `Claim-vs-Strike-Hindi-VO-female.mp3`.
`make_vo.py` rebuilds them (`python make_vo.py hi-IN-MadhurNeural +8% out.mp3`).
`script-whatsapp.txt` is the same script formatted for WhatsApp, with alternate hooks and a caption.

| Time | Voice (Hinglish) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Ek galti, aur aapka channel ja sakta hai. | ⚠️ 1 GALTI = CHANNEL GONE? |
| 0:03 | Woh galti hai, copyright claim aur strike ko ek samajhna. | CLAIM ≠ STRIKE |
| 0:07 | Claim matlab, aapke video mein kisi aur ka gaana ya clip hai. Channel safe hai. Bas us video ki kamai owner ko ja sakti hai, ya video block ho sakta hai. | CLAIM = Channel safe ✅ |
| 0:20 | Strike matlab, owner ne YouTube se video hatwa diya, aur channel pe strike lag gaya. Cricket jaisa samjho: claim mein run kate, strike mein wicket gira. 90 din mein 3 wicket, toh channel band ho sakta hai. | 🏏 Claim = run kate, Strike = wicket gira. 3 strikes in 90 din = channel khatre mein |
| 0:34 | Ab do galtiyan mat karna. Video delete karne se strike nahi hatta. Aur bina rights ke dispute kiya, toh claim strike ban sakta hai. | ❌ Video delete ≠ strike khatam. ❌ Bina rights ke dispute = strike ka risk |
| 0:44 | Samajh nahi aa raha kya karein? 'COPYRIGHT' likh ke DM karo. Hum sach-sach batayenge. | DM "COPYRIGHT" |
