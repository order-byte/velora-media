# Reel script: Copyright Claim vs Strike

Value reel, about 1:45 when read at a natural pace. Written after the client's feedback: simple
words, no jargon, a friendly tone, researched facts, and at least one minute long.

Voiceover demos: `Claim-vs-Strike-Hindi-VO.mp3` (male voice) and `Claim-vs-Strike-Hindi-VO-female.mp3`.
`make_vo.py` rebuilds them (`python make_vo.py hi-IN-MadhurNeural +8% out.mp3`).

| Time | Voice (Hinglish) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Video pe 'copyright' aaya, aur dil baith gaya? Ruko. Pehle yeh dekho, claim hai ya strike? Kyunki dono mein bahut bada farak hai. | CLAIM ya STRIKE? |
| 0:09 | Zyada tar creators dono ko ek hi samajhte hain, aur ghabra ke galat step le lete hain. | |
| 0:15 | Pehle CLAIM. Matlab, aapke video mein kisi aur ka gaana ya clip mila, aur uska owner keh raha hai, 'yeh hissa mera hai.' Aapka channel safe hai. Asar sirf us video pe padta hai. Aksar uski kamai owner ko jaane lagti hai, ya video block ho sakta hai. | CLAIM = channel safe ✅, asar sirf us video pe |
| 0:33 | Ab STRIKE. Yeh tab lagta hai, jab owner seedha YouTube se kehta hai, 'yeh video hatao.' Video hat jaata hai, aur channel pe strike lag jaata hai. Cricket ki tarah samjho: claim mein sirf run kate, aap abhi bhi crease pe ho. Strike matlab, wicket gira. Aur 90 din mein 3 wicket, toh channel band ho sakta hai. Ek strike 90 din mein khatam hota hai, lekin tabhi, jab aap YouTube ka 'Copyright School' poora karo. | Cricket graphic. 3 strikes in 90 din = channel khatre mein |
| 1:03 | Ab do badi galtiyan. Pehli: strike aate hi video delete karna. Isse strike nahi hatta. Haan, kabhi-kabhi YouTube email mein 7 din deta hai, video khud hatane ke liye. Toh email dhyaan se padho. Doosri: har claim pe dispute daalna. Agar gaana ya clip sach mein aapka nahi hai, toh owner video hatwa sakta hai, aur claim seedha strike ban jaata hai. | ❌ Video delete ≠ strike khatam. ❌ Bina rights ke dispute = strike ka risk |
| 1:31 | Toh agli baar ghabrao mat. Pehle check karo, claim hai ya strike. Aur agar samajh nahi aa raha ki aage kya karein, toh humein 'COPYRIGHT' likh ke DM karo. Hum pehle aapki situation samjhenge, phir sach batayenge ki kya ho sakta hai. | DM "COPYRIGHT" |
