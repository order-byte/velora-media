"Ek galti, aur aapka channel ja sakta hai."
📱 _On screen:_ ⚠️ 1 GALTI = CHANNEL GONE?

*2️⃣ HOOK REVEAL* (0:03 – 0:07)
"Woh galti hai, copyright claim aur strike ko ek samajhna."
📱 _On screen:_ CLAIM ≠ STRIKE

*3️⃣ VALUE: CLAIM* (0:07 – 0:20)
"Claim matlab, aapke video mein kisi aur ka gaana ya clip hai. Channel safe hai. Bas us video ki kamai owner ko ja sakti hai, ya video block ho sakta hai."
📱 _On screen:_ CLAIM = Channel safe ✅

*4️⃣ VALUE: STRIKE* (0:20 – 0:34)
"Strike matlab, owner ne YouTube se video hatwa diya, aur channel pe strike lag gaya.
Cricket jaisa samjho. Claim mein run kate, strike mein wicket gira. 90 din mein 3 wicket, toh channel band ho sakta hai."
📱 _On screen:_ 🏏 Claim = run kate | Strike = wicket gira
📱 _On screen:_ ⚠️ 3 strikes in 90 din = channel khatre mein

*5️⃣ VALUE: 2 galtiyan* (0:34 – 0:44)
"Ab do galtiyan mat karna. Video delete karne se strike nahi hatta. Aur bina rights ke dispute kiya, toh claim strike ban sakta hai."
📱 _On screen:_ ❌ Video delete ≠ Strike khatam
📱 _On screen:_ ❌ Bina rights ke dispute = Strike ka risk
