🎬 *REEL SCRIPT: Copyright Claim vs Copyright Strike*
*Format:* Value reel | *Duration:* ~1:45 | *Language:* Hindi (Hinglish)
*Style:* Talking head + on-screen text

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*1️⃣ HOOK* (0:00 – 0:09)
"Video pe 'copyright' aaya, aur dil baith gaya? Ruko. Pehle yeh dekho, claim hai ya strike? Kyunki dono mein bahut bada farak hai."
📱 _On screen:_ CLAIM ya STRIKE? 🤔

*2️⃣ PROBLEM* (0:09 – 0:15)
"Zyada tar creators dono ko ek hi samajhte hain, aur ghabra ke galat step le lete hain."

*3️⃣ VALUE: CLAIM kya hai* (0:15 – 0:33)
"Pehle CLAIM. Matlab, aapke video mein kisi aur ka gaana ya clip mila, aur uska owner keh raha hai, 'yeh hissa mera hai.'
Aapka channel safe hai. Asar sirf us video pe padta hai. Aksar uski kamai owner ko jaane lagti hai, ya video block ho sakta hai."
📱 _On screen:_ CLAIM = Channel safe ✅ | Asar sirf us video pe

*4️⃣ VALUE: STRIKE kya hai* (0:33 – 1:03)
"Ab STRIKE. Yeh tab lagta hai, jab owner seedha YouTube se kehta hai, 'yeh video hatao.' Video hat jaata hai, aur channel pe strike lag jaata hai.
Cricket ki tarah samjho. Claim mein sirf run kate, aap abhi bhi crease pe ho. Strike matlab, wicket gira. Aur 90 din mein 3 wicket, toh channel band ho sakta hai.
Ek strike 90 din mein khatam hota hai, lekin tabhi, jab aap YouTube ka 'Copyright School' poora karo."
📱 _On screen:_ 🏏 Claim = run kate | Strike = wicket gira
📱 _On screen:_ ⚠️ 3 strikes in 90 din = channel khatre mein

*5️⃣ VALUE: 2 badi galtiyan* (1:03 – 1:31)
"Ab do badi galtiyan.
Pehli, strike aate hi video delete karna. Isse strike nahi hatta. Haan, kabhi-kabhi YouTube email mein 7 din deta hai, video khud hatane ke liye. Toh email dhyaan se padho.
Doosri, har claim pe dispute daalna. Agar gaana ya clip sach mein aapka nahi hai, toh owner video hatwa sakta hai, aur claim seedha strike ban jaata hai."
📱 _On screen:_ ❌ Video delete ≠ Strike khatam
📱 _On screen:_ ❌ Bina rights ke dispute = Strike ka risk

*6️⃣ SERVICE BRIDGE* (1:31 – 1:36)
"Toh agli baar ghabrao mat. Pehle check karo, claim hai ya strike."

*7️⃣ CTA* (1:36 – 1:45)
"Aur agar samajh nahi aa raha ki aage kya karein, toh humein 'COPYRIGHT' likh ke DM karo. Hum pehle aapki situation samjhenge, phir sach batayenge ki kya ho sakta hai."
📱 _On screen:_ DM karein: *COPYRIGHT* 📩
