Copyright ki galatfehmiyan (reel, under 1 minute)

HOOK OPTIONS (pick one)

1. Paanch second ka gaana lagaya. Neeche credit bhi diya. "No copyright intended" bhi likha. Phir bhi channel pe strike aa gaya. Kyun?

2. Agar aap bhi apne video mein kisi aur ka gaana ya clip lagate ho, aur sochte ho ki credit de diya toh safe ho, toh yeh video poora dekhna.

3. Ek chhoti si galatfehmi, aur saalon ki mehnat se bana channel khatre mein aa sakta hai. Aur yeh galti bahut saare creators karte hain.

4. Credit dene se copyright khatam nahi hota. Yeh baat jitni jaldi samajh lo, aapka channel utna safe rahega.

5. Kya aapko bhi lagta hai ki "No copyright intended" likh do, toh strike nahi aayega? Toh aaj yeh galatfehmi khatam karte hain.

SCRIPT

Copyright ko lekar teen galatfehmiyan hain, jinki wajah se channel pe strike aata hai.

Pehli, "Maine credit de diya." Credit dena permission nahi hai. Permission sirf owner de sakta hai.

Doosri, "No copyright intended" likh dena. Is line se aapko koi right nahi milta. Yeh YouTube khud kehta hai.

Teesri, "Bas do second ka clip hai." Do second ka ho, tab bhi kisi aur ka hai. Us pe bhi claim ya strike aa sakta hai.

Seedhi baat yaad rakho. Jo cheez aapne khud nahi banayi, uske liye permission chahiye.

Claim ya strike aa chuka hai, aur samajh nahi aa raha kya karein? Toh "COPYRIGHT" likh ke DM karo. Hum sach-sach batayenge.
