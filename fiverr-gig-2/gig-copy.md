**FAQ** (9 questions)

1. **What does high-fidelity (HiFi) mean?**
   We rebuild your interface in After Effects instead of recording the screen. Text stays sharp in 4K, motion is smooth, and we can move the camera, highlight a feature or hide anything that isn't finished yet.

2. **Do I need finished UI screens?**
   No. If the product is still changing, we can design UI-inspired screens from your wireframes or Figma file and swap in the real screens later.

3. **Can you make a video from just an idea?**
   Yes. Tell me what the product does and who it's for. We'll write the script and plan the visuals with you before any animation starts.

4. **How long should my video be?**
   Shorter usually works better. Feature spots and ads: 15 to 30 seconds. Launch videos: 45 to 60 seconds. Full product demos: 90 seconds to 3 minutes.

5. **What if our product changes after launch?**
   We build every video in scenes, so a new screen or feature can be swapped in later without starting over. Message me when you need an update.

6. **Can I get vertical versions for social media?**
   Yes. Launch Film includes a 9:16 version. For the other packages, add the vertical version extra.

7. **How many revisions are included?**
   3 in every package. Script and storyboard changes are free until you approve them.

8. **Can you deliver fast?**
   Yes. Feature Spot can be ready in 1 day with the extra fast option. For bigger videos, message me with your deadline first.

9. **My project doesn't fit a package. What now?**
   Every product is different. Send me the details and I'll make you a custom offer.
