AMINUR REX SFX PACK
===================

Thanks for buying! Here's how to use your sounds.

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip this pack anywhere on your computer or phone.
2. Open the folder for the kind of sound you need (Whooshes, Impacts, Risers...).
3. Drag a sound onto your timeline in Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve,
   After Effects, CapCut, Alight Motion, Filmora or any editor that accepts audio files.

LICENSE
- Use these sounds in your own videos: YouTube, Instagram Reels, Shorts, ads and
  client projects, including monetized videos.
- Please don't resell, re-upload or share the sound files themselves.
